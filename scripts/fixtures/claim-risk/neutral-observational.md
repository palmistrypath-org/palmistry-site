title: "Fixture: neutral observational and editorial phrasing"
---

Cheiro (1916) describes a forked life line as traditionally associated with a change in direction later in life.
This marking may suggest a shift in circumstances, though Palmistry Path does not treat it as a prediction.
Benham reads the same feature differently, and the two traditions are noted here rather than reconciled.
To compare how far the line travels across this palm, hold both hands side by side under even light.
