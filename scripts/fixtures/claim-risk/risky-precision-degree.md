A long head line travels noticeably farther than average across the palm, while a short one ends before the central zone of the palm.
Some readers say the line simply reaches the central zone and stops there.
A very long line indicates a broader range of interests the longer it runs, and the shorter the line, the less curiosity it is said to reflect.
