## 2026-08-13 - Curriculum Wave 3E + 3F the 25-lesson core is complete
- **The approved 25-lesson core curriculum is complete.** Module totals are now **Foundations 5, Lines 6, Mounts 8, Minor Lines & Synthesis 6**. Remaining curriculum work in `ROADMAP.md` is P2-or-later polish and is not part of the core.
- **`advanced/01` now genuinely teaches the Sun and Mercury lines**, and is retitled **The Minor Lines: Sun and Mercury** (route unchanged, duration 7→13). It had named four minor lines and promised each "its own full lesson", but the Sun and Mercury lessons did not exist and were never going to — an independent competency test had already demoted both from P0 to P1, with the remedy being to revise this lesson rather than add two. Relationship lines and the Girdle of Venus are now brief forward-pointing orientation, which is honest because their own lessons follow immediately. **No separate Sun or Mercury lesson was created.**
- The Sun/Mercury material was built from the already Batch-2C-corrected `blog/beginner/sun-line.md` and `mercury-line.md`, so **no new quotation entered the corpus** and no Gettings/West/Fincham passage was promoted to a quotation. Cheiro's severe reading of an absent Sun line is stated as his and not softened; the gentler reading is labelled as the site's own; Benham is not credited with reassurance about absence he never gave. On the Mercury line the genuine disagreement is stated precisely — Cheiro reads absence as favourable, Benham does not read absence at all — the historical health claims are reported unsanitised including Cheiro dating death from the line, the site's refusal to read a crease for health is stated separately, and **the rejected medical reading was not replaced with an invented symbolic one**.
- **Moved the Simian lesson** from Lines to Minor Lines & Synthesis (`order: 4`, difficulty beginner→intermediate). This is the one planned existing-lesson URL change in the target curriculum. Lines is left at exactly 6 lessons and needed no edits. Batch 3B's source corrections are intact — 1–3% prevalence, *ekāgratā* as genuine Sanskrit but not classical hand-reading doctrine, no unsupported Vedic provenance. The generic chained/faint definition that Batch 3C deferred is removed; the lesson now applies the Line Quality vocabulary and links to the lesson that owns it.
- **Canonical Simian route is `/learn/advanced/simian-line`; the old `/learn/lines/06-simian-line` still resolves.** A hand-written static page at `src/pages/learn/lines/06-simian-line.astro` outranks the dynamic lesson route and carries a meta refresh, the canonical to the new URL, `noindex, follow`, and a Pagefind-ignored `<main id="main-content">`. Listing the path in `NOINDEX_PATHS` keeps it out of the sitemap and makes `audit:indexability` assert all of it on every build. **`astro.config.mjs` was deliberately not touched**, since a parallel technical branch may need it. Internal links repointed in `m-line-palmistry.md`, `simian-line.md`, `glossary.astro`, and `seo-content-roadmap.md`.
- **Added Combining What You See** (`src/content/lessons/advanced/combining-what-you-see.mdx`, route `/learn/advanced/combining-what-you-see`, `order: 5`), closing the curriculum audit's **last open P0**. It teaches observation before synthesis, weighting by structural authority, repetition over isolated evidence, agreement as corroboration rather than new information, genuine versus vague contradiction, leaving a contradiction unresolved, active/passive comparison as context, and where the system runs out. **Weighting is explicitly editorial judgement, not arithmetic** — no points, no scoring, no numeric tiers.
- The lesson carries **one worked reading** on a described fictional hand, with no photograph, no generated image and no external credit. It is designed to demonstrate what it teaches: four independent features converge on analytical/communicative intelligence, four others point at reserve and containment, and that contradiction is **deliberately left standing** rather than reconciled. The fragmentary girdle of Venus is down-weighted out loud because nothing else on the hand echoes it, and the synthesis names two questions the reading cannot answer.
- **Revised How to Give a Reading** to `order: 6`, removing the interpretive-sequence, weighting and holding-contradictions sections it had been carrying — the new lesson owns those now — and replacing them with a short pointer. Ethics, careful language, what to say when you don't know, and how to close are **preserved verbatim** and are now the bulk of the lesson rather than its last third. Section order was left alone deliberately, to avoid disturbing the ethics prose.
- **Established the practice layer.** `src/components/Practice.astro` marks each lesson's hands-on exercise, and **all 25 lessons carry one**, with no pedagogical exception needed. Eleven lessons wrapped an existing dedicated exercise section and three wrapped a trailing prompt that already functioned as one, both essentially unchanged. Nine — the seven individual mounts, the heart line and the simian line — ended in synthesis prose with no exercise and received a short new observation task drawn only from what that lesson already teaches.
- **Added four module-end checkpoints** via `src/components/Checkpoint.astro` and `CheckpointItem.astro`, on the final lesson of each module: Active and Passive Hand, Fate Line, Mars, and How to Give a Reading. Four or five applied prompts each, answers behind native `<details>`/`<summary>`, favouring application over recall, each stating plainly that revisiting a lesson carries no penalty.
- **No accounts, no progress tracking, no localStorage, no backend, no scoring and no pass/fail exist anywhere in the practice or checkpoint layer.** All three components are purely presentational with no JavaScript; keyboard accessibility comes from the native disclosure element and the existing global focus-visible rule. Nothing credentials a learner. **No printable worksheets or PDF pipeline were added**, and no systematic outbound "go deeper" link system was introduced — `relatedArticle` behaviour is unchanged.
- `scripts/audit-schema.mjs` now **exempts `noindex` pages from required-JSON-LD-type checks**. A page withheld from the index has nothing to declare, and emitting `LearningResource` on the Simian redirect stub would advertise one lesson at two URLs. The accessibility guardrail was left unconditional and the stub was written to satisfy it instead.
- Recorded a **structural gap worth knowing**: nothing in the build enforces unique or sequential `order` values within a module. `getStaticPaths` sorts by `order` and tolerates duplicates silently, so a collision yields two lessons numbered the same with no build error and no audit failure. This wave hit exactly that, and caught it only by checking module ordering explicitly.
- Also corrected a claim introduced while wrapping the mount exercises: the Mercury exercise had called Apollo that mount's only true neighbour, when Upper Mars borders it directly below on the percussion edge, as the Mars and Luna lessons both state.

## 2026-08-13 - Remediation Batch 3D the thumb and the fingers
- Added **The Thumb and the Fingers** as the fourth lesson of Foundations (`src/content/lessons/foundations/thumb-and-fingers.mdx`, route `/learn/foundations/thumb-and-fingers`). It teaches the structural pass the course previously walked past: thumb length against a source-verified landmark, thumb setting, the two thumb sections, resting opening angle, tip-joint flexibility, the one-time planetary finger mapping, per-finger comparison landmarks, base arch, resting spacing, and lean. The core curriculum is now **24 lessons**; Foundations has **5**.
- The new lesson carries an unnumbered filename with `order: 4`; `04-active-and-passive-hand.mdx` moved to `order: 5` and remains the Foundations closing lesson. **No file renamed and no URL changed.**
- **Thumb length is now taught from a verified landmark.** Benham (1900) gives it: a thumb of ordinary length reaches about the middle of the index finger's base segment, with the thumb held against the side of the hand — plus his own caveat that a low or high setting confounds the measurement. Cheiro gives no landmark at all; the ambiguous "reaches to or beyond the first joint" wording in the blog is not his and is not reproduced.
- **Will and reason are taught in three separated moves**: the physical proportion, then the historical reading, then the site's boundary. Benham's own framing is quoted — he adopted the two names "for brevity" — and the lesson states plainly that they are traditional labels for a proportion, not measurements of anyone's intelligence, willpower, self-control, judgement, or character. **No invented modern meaning replaces the historical one.**
- Deliberately out of core: the clubbed or "murderer's" thumb and all sensational variants, thumb-tip and fingertip typologies, the three phalange zones, knotted-versus-smooth joints, per-finger meaning tables, finger flexibility as a separate test, and nails. Nails carry a one-sentence boundary that does not imply a future lesson. No Indian or Chinese equivalence is claimed; the lesson states that the will/reason frame and the planetary finger names are Western constructions.
- **Finger prominence is not raw length.** The middle finger is longest on almost every hand, so the lesson teaches each finger against Benham's own per-finger landmarks and defines prominence as departure from those, not as being longer than a neighbour.
- Retitled `foundations/03-hand-shapes.mdx` to **Hand Shape, Texture, and Flexibility** (route unchanged, duration 8→10) and added skin texture, consistency under pressure, and whole-hand flexibility as a **separate qualifying layer placed after the four types**. The elemental classification still rests on two inputs and only two; the section says so explicitly and states that these observations never change a hand's type. Boundaries are stated on the page: texture is not line quality, consistency is not mount firmness, whole-hand flexibility is not the thumb-tip joint.
- Added the historical clarification that d'Arpentigny (1843) supplied the earlier Western shape typology, that the four-element framing came later, and that **Cheiro and Benham did not use the system this lesson teaches**.
- Revised `foundations/02-how-to-read-a-palm.mdx`: step three compressed to an observation prompt now that lesson 3 teaches the interpretations; a new thumb/finger step added so the course's own reading sequence no longer omits a layer it teaches (steps renumbered to seven); the claim that palm-to-finger ratio is "the foundation of every major hand classification system" narrowed to what the repo supports; "associations across traditions" removed; and the Sun/Apollo inversion corrected to match the Mounts module.
- Corrected the forward reference in `mounts/03-mount-of-jupiter.mdx`, which pointed at "the finger lessons that follow" — after 3D that lesson exists and precedes the Mounts module.
- Repointed `relatedLesson` in `thumb-meaning-palmistry.md` and `finger-shapes-palmistry.md` to the new lesson. The new lesson's `relatedArticle` is the finger article, chosen because it carries no quotation-marked source passages; the thumb article's unresolved Gettings quotation remains a separate backlog item.
