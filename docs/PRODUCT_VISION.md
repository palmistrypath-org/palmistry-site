# Product Vision — Palmistry Path

## Purpose
Palmistry Path is an educational learning platform for beginner-to-advanced palm reading. It teaches palmistry as a structured observational and interpretive tradition, with intellectual honesty about its cultural and historical nature.

It is not a fortune-telling service, predictive science, or substitute for professional advice.

## Product principles
1. **Trust before monetization.** Build a useful, well-structured body of learning content first.
2. **Progressive learning.** Help a beginner move from foundations into lines, mounts, and advanced synthesis instead of presenting an undifferentiated article archive.
3. **Source-grounded interpretation.** Do not invent meanings or collapse different traditions into false consensus.
4. **Atmosphere without hype.** The visual experience can feel mystical/ancient; the writing remains educational and grounded.
5. **Interactivity must be earned by content.** Add exercises, hand maps, journals, progress, or membership only when the underlying content justifies them.

## Editorial stance
Preferred phrasing includes:
- “traditionally associated with…”
- “often interpreted as…”
- “may suggest…”

Avoid presenting palmistry interpretations as objective facts or predictions. Never use palmistry to make medical, legal, financial, or relationship-advice claims.

### Traditions
Palmistry Path may draw from Western palmistry, Indian/Hasta Samudrika traditions, and Chinese palmistry. When traditions disagree, say so. Brief, accurate references are preferable to forced comparison or over-explanation.

Historical gendered hand conventions may be described honestly in historical/tradition-comparison content. Practical instruction generally uses active/passive or dominant/non-dominant framing unless the historical distinction materially changes the interpretation.

## Audience
Primary audience: curious beginners who want a clear path into palmistry without sensational claims. The platform should also remain useful as a reference for readers progressing into more nuanced interpretation.

## Brand
- Name: **Palmistry Path**
- Domain: **palmistrypath.com**
- Tagline: **“Read the lines. Discover the path.”**
- Tone: educational, grounded, curious, respectful, atmospheric
- Visual mood: **Editorial Mystic** — 70% clean editorial, 20% warm mystical atmosphere, and 10% premium decorative accent, without sacrificing legibility or accessibility
- Established typography in the implementation: Cinzel for headings and Lora for body copy
- Established palette centers on warm ivory/parchment surfaces, charcoal text, muted teal, warm sand, and restrained gold. `docs/visual-system.md` and the implementation are authoritative for exact semantic tokens and usage rules.

## Learning/product direction
Current implementation already supports a structured lesson library. Longer-term ideas may include interactive hand maps, observation/practice exercises, glossary cross-linking, reading journals, progress tracking, and eventually paid products or membership—but these are directions, not automatically approved tasks.

Avoid multiple-choice “single correct answer” pedagogy for interpretive palmistry when observational practice would better reflect the subject.

## Monetization guardrails
Monetization can include guides/courses, affiliate content, email products, and tasteful advertising, but should not undermine trust or lead the product ahead of its content. Do not add fake testimonials, fake user counts, or unearned social proof.

## Accessibility and product quality
The site should remain mobile-first, semantically structured, accessible, fast, and easy to navigate. Mystical styling must never reduce readability or clarity.
