# PP-RELAY-046 Director Evidence — Life Line Curvature Boundary

Date: 2026-08-23

## Question
Does approved evidence support a standalone `curved vs straight Life Line` interpretation independent of the Mount of Venus?

## Verified boundary
- William G. Benham, *The Laws of Scientific Hand Reading* (1900), treats the Life Line's path relative to the thumb as changing the territory/operation of the Mount of Venus. The verified formulation already used in Palmistry Path is that a Life Line lying close to the thumb reduces the size of the Mount of Venus and checks the operation of that mount.
- Current approved Palmistry Path Life Line/Venus material therefore treats wide/narrow arc as a paired line-plus-mount observation, not as a separate mount-free curved/straight personality axis.
- An independent Director search of accessible public-domain/classical material did not establish a separate named-source `curved Life Line = X / straight Life Line = Y` interpretation that can safely be detached from the Venus boundary.

## Director decision
The backlog search intent remains useful, but the article must not manufacture a symmetrical curved/straight reading. It may answer the query by:
1. teaching comparative observation of the path without a universal normal curve, fixed angle, or population cutoff;
2. explaining that in the verified Benham evidence, the meaningful historical distinction is how the Life Line's arc changes the territory of the Mount of Venus;
3. explicitly stating that Palmistry Path does not currently have approved evidence for a separate mount-independent `straight Life Line` meaning;
4. directing readers to the Mount of Venus material for the historical paired interpretation;
5. excluding health, lifespan, vitality-as-medical-fact, deterministic personality, prevalence, and stronger-with-degree claims.

This is an evidence-bounded reframing, not permission to infer a new interpretation from the absence of evidence.
