# PP-RELAY-048 Director Evidence — Cheiro Fate Line Break

## Source
Cheiro, *Palmistry for All* (1916), Chapter V / Plate XIII discussion of the Line of Destiny/Fate.

Director verification on 2026-08-24 used the public-domain transcription at classic-literature.net and corroborating Project Gutenberg-derived text surfaced in search.

## Verified evidence boundary
Cheiro states that when the Line of Fate is broken or made up in small pieces, he historically associated the career with troubles/breaks and lack of settled or continuous success.

Cheiro then gives a narrower exception/configuration: when one side of the broken Fate Line begins before the other ends (an overlapping break), he historically associated it with a complete change in surroundings and position. He further says that if the new line is good and straight, he associated the new segment with advancement in position from the point where the second line begins.

## What this evidence DOES support for PP-RELAY-048
- A Fate Line break can be described observationally as an interruption/gap in the line.
- Cheiro may be named and historically attributed for his own break interpretation.
- The overlapping-break configuration may be described specifically as one segment beginning before the other ends.
- Cheiro may be attributed with reading that overlapping configuration as a change in surroundings/position.
- Cheiro's conditional advancement reading may be mentioned only if the condition is kept explicit: the new segment is described by him as good and straight.

## What this evidence DOES NOT support
- Do not generalize Cheiro's period wording into a modern rule that every Fate Line break means career change or life change.
- Do not claim an overlapping break universally means a smooth, planned, managed, beneficial, or gradual transition. Cheiro's verified wording is change in surroundings/position; preserve that narrower historical attribution.
- Do not claim a shifted restart means a new field, multiple sections mean multiple life chapters, or a resumed line has any generic meaning unless separately verified.
- Do not infer modern prevalence, consensus, frequency, or population norms.
- Do not use gap size as a severity scale.
- Do not use Cheiro's timing/date system in this task.
- Do not extend the evidence to health, danger, death, relationship, financial, or guaranteed outcome claims.
- Do not present Cheiro's historical claims as empirical fact or Palmistry Path policy.

## Drafting guidance
A useful narrow article is supportable. It should distinguish observation from interpretation, explain that Palmistry Path treats historical readings as tradition rather than prediction, and be explicit that the currently verified evidence supports a Cheiro-specific break/overlap reading rather than the broader legacy prose already present elsewhere in the site.
