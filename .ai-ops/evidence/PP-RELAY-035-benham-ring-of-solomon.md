## Verified evidence boundary
Benham identifies the Ring of Solomon as a minor line and describes a specific path: it begins between the Jupiter and Saturn fingers, runs downward around the Mount of Jupiter, and ends near the beginning of the Life line.

Benham interprets the marking within his historical system as connected with interest in occult studies and potential proficiency in them when other chirognomic indications also support that reading.

Benham additionally reports personal observational claims about the kinds of hands in which he saw the mark and about accompanying markings. Those are historically attributable to Benham but should not be generalized into modern prevalence, consensus, psychology, or empirical fact without additional evidence.
