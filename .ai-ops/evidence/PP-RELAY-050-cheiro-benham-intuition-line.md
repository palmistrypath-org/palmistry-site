# PP-RELAY-050 Director Evidence — Cheiro and Benham on the Line of Intuition

## Sources

Director verification on 2026-08-25 used these public-domain primary-text reproductions:

- Cheiro, *Palmistry for All* (1916), "The Line of Intuition," surfaced in the public-domain transcription/search record. Cheiro names the feature **the Line of Intuition**, describes a roughly semicircular course from the face of the Mount of Mercury to the Mount of Moon (or a form confined to the Mount of Moon), distinguishes it from the Hepatica/Line of Health, and historically associates it with a highly sensitive temperament, presentiments, inspiration, vivid dreams, intuitive impressions, and inspired speaking or writing.
- Cheiro, *Cheiro's Language of the Hand*, Chapter XIV, "The Via Lasciva and the Line of Intuition," public-domain scan at `https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/eb/Cheiro%27s_language_of_the_hand%3B_%28IA_cheiroslanguageo00chei_1%29.pdf`. Cheiro again names **the Line of Intuition**, places its near-semicircular course from Mercury toward Luna, says it may run through or with the Hepatica while remaining distinguishable, and historically associates it with an impressionable nature, sensitivity to surroundings and influences, presentiments concerning others, and vivid dreams or warnings.
- William G. Benham, *The Laws of Scientific Hand Reading* (1900/1901), "The Line of Intuition," public-domain transcription at `https://chestofbooks.com/new-age/palmistry/Hand-Reading/The-Line-Of-Intuition.html`. Benham places the line near the percussion, rising on the Mount of Moon, curving toward the Plain of Mars, and ending on or near the Mount of Mercury. He distinguishes it from the straighter Mercury line by its curve. He historically associates a well-marked line with intuitive impressions and sensitivity in estimating people; he says a deep line indicates more of the attributed faculty and a broken or defective one less.

These sources are historical palmistry texts, not empirical evidence. Their claims must be attributed to the named author and must not be converted into facts about cognition, paranormal ability, health, prediction, or outcomes.

## Verified evidence boundary

The evidence supports a narrow beginner article that:

- names the feature **Line of Intuition**;
- describes its observable curved or near-semicircular course along the outer/percussion side between the Luna and Mercury regions, noting the slightly different directional wording in Cheiro and Benham rather than inventing a single exact endpoint rule;
- distinguishes it observationally from the Mercury/Health line (Hepatica) using the named authors' curve-versus-line distinction, while acknowledging Cheiro says the markings can run through or alongside one another;
- attributes to Cheiro his historical associations with sensitivity to impressions/surroundings, presentiments, vivid dreams, inspiration, and intuitive expression;
- attributes to Benham his historical associations with intuitive impressions and sensitivity in estimating other people;
- may mention Benham's deep-versus-broken degree reading only as Benham's named historical claim, not as an empirical scale or Palmistry Path assessment;
- states separately that Palmistry Path treats these as historical interpretations and does not use the line to validate psychic ability, predict events, or guarantee that an impression is accurate.

## Important source disagreement / naming limit

The approved primary evidence consistently calls the feature **Line of Intuition**. It uses words such as `clairvoyance`, `psychic`, or `medium` inside historical interpretations, but it does **not** establish **Line of Clairvoyance**, **Psychic Line**, or another phrase as a verified alternate historical name.

The backlog's note that "line of clairvoyance" is the historical name came from unverified copyright-era source planning (Gettings/Fincham). Do not repeat that synonym claim in the article, title, description, headings, FAQ, source log, or metadata. It may be recorded only as an unsupported search phrase that this task intentionally declines to validate.

## What this evidence does not support

- No prevalence, rarity, percentage, population norm, or claim about which hand types usually carry the line. Cheiro's type-specific wording is not a population estimate and is unnecessary here.
- No claim that presence proves psychic, clairvoyant, mediumistic, supernatural, prophetic, diagnostic, empathic, or scientifically validated ability.
- No claim that absence means a person lacks intuition, sensitivity, imagination, or any capacity.
- No claim that an impression, hunch, dream, warning, or opinion is accurate, reliable, predictive, or actionable as fact. Benham's period claims about accuracy must not become Palmistry Path guidance.
- No medical, mental-health, nervous-system, sleep, legal, financial, relationship, danger, death, or life-event claim.
- No combination reading with the Head, Heart, Fate, Mercury, Life, or other lines; mounts; hand shape; finger shape; stars; crosses; islands; branches; forks; or endpoints. Benham supplies many such period combinations, but they are deliberately outside this bounded article because they introduce avoidable predictive, medical, and synthesis risk.
- No interpretation for islands, stars, crosses, branches, forks, doubled forms, interruptions, length, exact geometry, or direction beyond the bounded location distinction above.
- No stronger-with-degree extrapolation beyond Benham's explicit deep-versus-broken statement, and that statement may be omitted.
- No cross-tradition claim. This evidence establishes only two named Western authors.
- No Gettings, Fincham, West, Indian, Chinese, or contemporary-practitioner claim. The repository explicitly marks the relevant copyright-era quotations as unverified, and no approved evidence for those sources is supplied here.
- No direct quotation unless the worker independently checks the exact cited public-domain edition and records it verbatim; paraphrase is preferred.
