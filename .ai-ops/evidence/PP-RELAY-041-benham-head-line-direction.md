## Claim boundary established

1. **Straight Head Line → practical mentality:** supported. Benham explicitly says the straight Head line shows the operation of a practical mentality. This supports a narrow historical paraphrase such as “Benham associated a straight-running Head Line with practical thinking.” Do not inflate this into IQ, superiority, business success, or deterministic personality.

2. **Head Line sloping toward the Mount of Moon/Luna → imagination:** supported. Benham says a line running toward the Moon indicates the subject is not entirely material in ideas and has the power of imagination. This supports a narrow historical association with imagination and a contrast with purely practical/material thought.

3. **Creative/artistic stronger-slope rule:** **not established as a monotonic degree rule.** Benham notes writers, speakers, linguists, and examples of fiction/poetry writers among people with drooping Head Lines, including some lines extending far into the Mount. That supports mentioning creative/literary examples as Benham’s observations, but it does not establish “the steeper the slope, the more creative/artistic” or any fixed degree threshold.

4. **Strongly sloping = artistic/creative as a standalone deterministic meaning:** do not state this as a general rule from Benham. If retained at all, narrow it to Benham’s historical examples of writers/poets among sloping-to-Moon lines, clearly attributed and without prevalence or degree escalation.

5. **Clinical/insanity language in Benham:** historical and unsafe for modern instructional use. Do not carry it into Palmistry Path except in a source-history discussion with explicit historical framing and only if specifically authorized. It is unnecessary for this article.
