# PP-RELAY-058 — Benham Rascettes evidence boundary

Director verification date: 2026-08-28

## Verified source boundary

Public-domain William G. Benham, *The Laws of Scientific Hand Reading* (1900), Rascettes/Bracelets section was independently checked through an accessible transcription of the Benham text.

The verified Benham material supports only the following bounded historical claims for this task:

- Benham identifies the **Rascettes, or Bracelets** as lines crossing the wrist at the base of the hand.
- He says there may be three, and sometimes only one; this is an observation in his text, not a modern prevalence statistic.
- Benham reports that older palmists assigned roughly thirty years of life to each bracelet, but explicitly says he could not confirm that rule and did not use the bracelets as a longevity measure.
- He treats the first bracelet as the useful one and historically associates a deep first bracelet with a strong constitution; poorly marked/chained/broad-and-shallow forms receive the opposite historical reading.
- He also gives a historical interpretation to short branches rising from the Rascettes, associating them with upward striving/ambition.

Accessible verification source: `https://www.superphysics.org/research/benham/palmistry/part-2/chapter-14c/` (transcription labeled as Benham, chapter "Racettes and Lines of Travel").

## Safety and interpretation limits

- Benham's constitution/longevity statements are **historical palmistry claims**, not medical evidence. Palmistry Path must not present wrist creases as measuring health, vitality, fertility, disease risk, or lifespan.
- Do not revive the old per-bracelet lifespan arithmetic as a usable method. If mentioned, the important source fact is that Benham himself says he could not confirm it and did not use it.
- Do not infer a modern population norm from Benham's "sometimes three / may be one" wording.
- Do not infer that first/second/third bracelets correspond to modern health/wealth/happiness systems unless separately verified in an approved source.
- Do not import West (1998), Fincham (2005), commercial palmistry pages, Indian/Samudrik claims, or anonymous "modern palmists" as authority unless independently verified under repository source policy.
- Do not add reproductive/gynecological, disease, pregnancy, diagnostic, or medical advice claims from wrist-line shapes.
- Do not synthesize Rascette meanings with Life Line, mounts, travel lines, or other features unless the specific combined reading is independently established and authorized.

This evidence note is a bounded source aid for PP-RELAY-058, not authorization to publish claims beyond the limits above.
