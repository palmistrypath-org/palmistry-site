## Cheiro — *Palmistry for All* (1916)

Primary text: Project Gutenberg ebook 20480, `Palmistry for All` by Cheiro.

Director review of the searchable full text found:

- no occurrence of the word `teacher` in the book;
- Cheiro's square chapter calls the square the **Mark of Preservation** and describes preservation/escape meanings on lines;
- Cheiro's Mount of Jupiter chapter discusses Jupiter qualities, but the checked primary text does not name a `Teacher's Square` or assign a teaching interpretation to a square on Jupiter.

Primary text: https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/20480/pg20480-images.html

This directly contradicts modern web pages that attribute a named Teacher's Square / born-teacher reading to Cheiro. Those modern pages are discovery aids only and are not approved citation authority.

## Benham — *The Laws of Scientific Hand Reading* (1900)

The accessible Benham Mount of Jupiter transcription states that a square, along with several other favorable marks, **strengthens a Mount**. The verified section does not name a `Teacher's Square` or independently assign a teaching-specific interpretation to a square on Jupiter.

Accessible transcription used for this boundary check: https://www.superphysics.org/research/benham/palmistry/part-1/chapter-19-jupiter/

Google Books confirms the 1900 Benham edition and Mount of Jupiter chapter location, but no Director-verified primary/approved passage has been found that establishes the named feature or the teaching-specific meaning.

## Editorial conclusion for this task

Available approved evidence is sufficient to discuss:

- the Mount of Jupiter as a named palmistry mount under the repository's existing source policy;
- Cheiro's general square-as-preservation treatment;
- Benham's generic rule that a square may strengthen a mount.

Available approved evidence is **not sufficient** to claim that:

- `Teacher's Square` is a classical Cheiro or Benham term;
- Cheiro called a square on Jupiter the sign of a born teacher;
- Benham assigned teaching aptitude, patience, explanatory ability, mentoring skill, or educator career outcomes to a Jupiter square;
- a generic square-on-Jupiter rule can be synthesized into a teaching meaning by combining separate square and Jupiter interpretations;
- the named feature's prevalence, exact geometry, chronology, cross-tradition status, or popularity is established.

Modern commercial/SEO pages surfaced by search make several of those claims, but they are Tier 4 discovery material under Palmistry Path's editorial policy and cannot be used as citation authority.

For PP-RELAY-056, the safe resolution is therefore **do not publish a Teacher's Square article from the current evidence**. Reconcile the backlog item as source-unverified/deferred rather than asking the owner to supply a source or inventing a meaning.
