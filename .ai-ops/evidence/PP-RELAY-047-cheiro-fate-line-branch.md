# PP-RELAY-047 Director evidence — Cheiro Fate Line branching

Verified by the Director against two independent public-domain transcriptions of Cheiro, *Palmistry for All* (1916), Chapter V / Plate XIII material.

## Verified passage boundary

Cheiro explicitly describes a Fate Line configuration with **one branch on the Mount of Venus and the other on the Mount of Moon**. In Cheiro's historical system, he interprets that configuration as a career/destiny strongly influenced by romance and passion.

Primary-text locations independently checked:
- https://akirarabelais.com/o/thelibraryofbabel/cheiro/palmistry.html — Chapter V, around lines 639–646 in the indexed transcription.
- https://classic-literature.net/cheiro-1866-1936/palmistry-for-all/ — Chapter V, around lines 687–694 in the indexed transcription.

Both transcriptions contain the same Fate Line branch statement immediately before Plate XIII.

## What this evidence supports

- Cheiro specifically recognized a Fate Line divided into two branches in the lower/origin region, one associated with Venus and one with Moon.
- A beginner article may describe this as a **specific lower/base branching or fork-like configuration**, provided it clearly distinguishes this verified form from other fork locations.
- Cheiro historically associated this specific Venus/Moon split with romance/passion influencing the person's career or life direction.
- Palmistry Path may paraphrase that historical interpretation conservatively and explicitly attribute it to Cheiro.
